# Review — 31 October 2026

A hands-on pass over the installed `netget --mcp` (HTTP, Redis, PostgreSQL and Telnet servers,
a Redis client, manual intercepts, error paths) plus a check of the planning backlog against
source. Items are grouped into workstreams (W1…W20); each workstream is one agent on its own
branch, merged into `master` with `--no-ff`.

Legend: `[ ]` open · `[~]` in progress · `[x]` done (merged) · `[?]` needs a decision from the
maintainer · **(repro)** reproduced by hand on 31 Oct · **(backlog)** from the planning docs,
verified against source.
